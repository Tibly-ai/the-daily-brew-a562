The Brew Standard

How we craft your cup

Every morning requires an absolute commitment to detail. Here is how we ensure each sip is memorable.

01

Sourcing and Seasons

Coffee is a fresh agricultural fruit. We only buy green beans from the current crop cycle, partnering with small family co-ops that treat farmers with respect and pay above fair-trade standards.

02

Micro-Batch Roasting

We roast in small batches on our custom-tailored drum roaster. By tasting each profile repeatedly, we find the precise heat profile that highlights origin characteristics rather than hiding them under heavy smoke.

03

Precision Brewing

Our baristas measure coffee dose down to a tenth of a gram, checking water quality, temperatures, and extraction pressure multiple times every single morning. The result is pure harmony in your cup.
